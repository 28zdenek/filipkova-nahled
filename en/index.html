Czech accounting and a real business base in Břeclav.

We provide accounting services for Czech companies whose owners live or do business in Slovakia, Hungary, Austria or another country. Our office in Břeclav gives them a reliable point of contact in the Czech Republic.

We also offer a registered office address in Břeclav – on its own or together with our accounting services – and, when agreed, a shared office or meeting space.

CZECHIA SLOVAKIA AUSTRIA Břeclav Diagram, not to scale

If you are preparing a Czech company

We do not incorporate companies ourselves. We connect you with a cooperating Czech notary or lawyer who handles the legal incorporation. We then arrange the accounting setup, registrations within the agreed scope, payroll and ongoing accounting administration.

If you already own a Czech company

We can take over its accounting, payroll and routine tax compliance. We first review the current accounting records, business activities, VAT status, employees and cross-border transactions. We then agree on a safe handover process.

Our services

  • statutory accounting for Czech limited liability and joint-stock companies
  • VAT returns, related reports, financial statements and tax returns
  • payroll processing and employee administration
  • a registered office address in Břeclav
  • a shared office or meeting space when agreed
  • coordination with a Czech notary, lawyer, auditor or external tax adviser
  • communication with Czech authorities within the agreed scope and under a valid power of attorney

Tax compliance and specialist support

We handle tax returns and routine tax compliance as part of the accounting services we provide. For complex or non-standard cases, we cooperate with external Czech tax advisers.
